The Man Who Moved Continents
0:001:28

英 文 原 文 · 听 读
In 1912 a German meteorologist named Alfred Wegener suggested提出（观点） that the continents had once been joined and had since moved apart. He was not a geologist, which did not help. His evidence was good. The coast of Brazil matched the coast of Africa the way two halves of a torn page match吻合. The same fossil reptile, a freshwater animal that could not have swum an ocean, turned up on both sides. Rock layers and mountain chains ran off one continent and continued on another. Ancient glaciers had left their scratches in places that are now hot. What he could not supply was a force力. He imagined the continents ploughing through the ocean floor, and physicists proved证明 that nothing available was strong enough to push them. Geologists rejected the whole idea, and for forty years the word drift was a joke in the field. Here is the part usually left out: they were right to reject否定 his mechanism. It really was impossible不可能的. Wegener was right about what had happened and wrong about how it happened, and neither side could accept接受 that both could be true at once. The answer came after his death — he died on the Greenland ice in 1930 — when the sea floor itself turned out to be spreading, carrying the continents along on top of it. He had never needed to push them through anything. His facts had not been enough to convince说服 anyone, because a fact with no mechanism behind it is not yet science. That rule cost him his career, and it is still a good rule.
参 考 译 文
1912 年，一位名叫阿尔弗雷德·魏格纳的德国气象学家提出：各大陆曾经连在一起，后来才彼此分开。他不是地质学家——这一点对他没什么帮助。 他的证据是好的。巴西的海岸与非洲的海岸吻合，就像一页纸被撕成两半那样吻合。同一种化石爬行动物——一种淡水动物，根本不可能游过一片大洋——在大洋两岸都出现了。岩层和山脉从一块大陆延伸出去，又在另一块大陆上接着往下走。古代冰川把擦痕留在了如今炎热的地方。 他拿不出来的，是一个力。他设想大陆是「犁」过洋底的，而物理学家证明：当时能想到的任何力都不足以推动它们。地质学家否定了整套说法，此后四十年里，「漂移」这个词在这一行里就是个笑话。 下面这一层通常被略去不提：他们拒绝他的机制是对的。 那个机制确实不可能成立。魏格纳对的是「发生了什么」，错的是「怎么发生的」；而两边都没能接受——这两件事可以同时成立。 答案是在他死后才到来的——1930 年他死在格陵兰的冰上——人们发现，是洋底本身在扩张，驮着大陆一起走。他从来就不需要推着大陆穿过任何东西。他的事实没能说服任何人，因为背后没有机制的事实，还算不上科学。这条规矩让他搭上了一生，而它仍然是条好规矩。
