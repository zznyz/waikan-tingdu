Ghosts of the Reef

英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Coral reefs are among the most colourful places in the ocean, alive with fish and other creatures. Yet in many parts of the world, these reefs are losing their colour and turning a ghostly white. Scientists call this process "coral bleaching," and it offers a striking lesson in how small changes in the environment can upset a whole natural system. A coral reef is built by tiny sea animals. Inside their bodies live smaller algae, and the two depend on each other. The algae absorb吸收 sunlight and turn it into food, sharing this energy with the coral and giving the reef its bright colours. In return, the coral offers the algae a safe shelter庇护所. This close partnership keeps the whole reef in balance平衡. The trouble begins when the sea grows too warm. Under such stress, the coral can no longer survive幸存 alongside its partner, so it forces the algae out and releases释放 them into the water. Without the algae, the coral loses both its food and its colour, and only its pale skeleton remains. Rising sea temperatures now threaten威胁 reefs across the globe. A bleached coral is not yet dead, and if cooler conditions return in time, it may slowly recover恢复. But if the heat lasts too long, the coral dies, and the countless species物种 that rely on the reef lose their home as well. In this way, a warming ocean harms far more than one animal; it can unravel a whole web of life shaped over countless years.

珊瑚礁是海洋中最绚丽的地方之一，然而在许多海域，这些珊瑚礁正褪去颜色、惨白如鬼影，科学家称之为"珊瑚白化"。珊瑚礁由微小的海洋动物建造，它们体内住着更小的藻类：藻类吸收阳光、转化为食物，把能量分享给珊瑚，也赋予其明亮色彩；作为回报，珊瑚为藻类提供安全的庇护所，这种伙伴关系让珊瑚礁维持平衡。麻烦始于海水过热：珊瑚再也无法与伙伴共同存活，于是把藻类驱逐、释放到水中，只剩一副苍白的骨骼。如今升高的海水温度正威胁着全球珊瑚礁。白化的珊瑚尚未死亡，若凉爽环境及时回归，或许还能慢慢恢复；但若高温持续太久，珊瑚便会死去，无数依赖珊瑚礁为生的物种也将失去家园。
