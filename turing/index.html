In the Second World War, German forces sent their orders using Enigma, a machine that turned ordinary messages into meaningless letters. Its settings changed every day, and the possible combinations组合 were so many that testing them one by one would take longer than a lifetime. To the Germans, the code seemed impossible to crack破译. At Bletchley Park, north of London, a young mathematician named Alan Turing thought otherwise. He reasoned that its greatest strength was also its weakness: because German operators often began messages with predictable可预测的 phrases — a daily weather report — the endless possibilities could be narrowed down. Turing and his colleague designed a machine that hunted for these patterns规律 at a speed no team of people could match. Thanks to the intelligence it produced, historians estimate that the war in Europe was shortened缩短 by around two years, saving millions of lives. His contribution贡献 went further still: his ideas about what a machine could compute laid the foundations of modern computer science. Yet his own country treated him cruelly残酷地（cruel adj. 残酷的）. In 1952 he was convicted simply for being gay, then legal grounds for prosecution in Britain, and he died two years later at forty-one. Official recognition认可 came only decades on — an apology in 2009, a royal pardon in 2013.
