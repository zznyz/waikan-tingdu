Wilbur and Orville Wright repaired修理 bicycles in a small shop. They had no university degree学位 and no money from the government政府, but they had a question that would not leave them alone: why could birds turn in the air when machines could not? They watched birds for hours and noticed注意到 that a bird twists the tips of its wings. So the brothers built wings that could twist too. Then they tested more than two hundred shapes in a wind tunnel隧道（wind tunnel 风洞） they made themselves. On a cold, windy morning in December 1903, on an empty beach, Orville lay flat on the lower wing and the machine rose into the air. The flight飞行 lasted twelve seconds and covered 36 metres, shorter than the wing of a modern现代的 plane. Only five people stood watching. Most newspapers refused拒绝 to print the news, and it took five more years before the world finally believed them.

威尔伯·莱特和奥维尔·莱特在一间小店里修自行车。他们没有大学学位，也没有政府的一分钱，却有一个甩不掉的问题：鸟能在空中转弯，机器为什么不能？他们看了几个小时的鸟，注意到鸟会扭动翅膀的尖端。于是兄弟俩造出了也能扭动的机翼。接着他们在自己搭的风洞里试了两百多种形状。1903 年 12 月一个又冷又刮风的早上，在一片空荡荡的海滩上，奥维尔趴在下层机翼上，那台机器升上了天。这次飞行持续了十二秒，飞出 36 米——比一架现代飞机的机翼还短。当时只有五个人站在旁边看。大多数报纸拒绝刊登这条消息，又过了五年，世界才终于信了他们。
