英 文 原 文 · 听 读
More than 150 years ago, a brave British woman named Florence Nightingale became a nurse护士. During a war, she was sent to a hospital full of injured soldiers士兵. Each night she walked between the beds with a small lamp, so people called her "the Lady with the Lamp. Soon she noticed something strange: far more men were dying from disease疾病 than from any wound伤口. The hospital was dirty肮脏的, with bad water and foul air. Nightingale believed that clean rooms could keep the men alive, but few would listen. So she did something new: she counted every death and drew a colourful chart图表. It made the truth easy to see — most men died from illness, not from battle. The picture helped to prove证明 her point, and leaders finally cleaned the hospital. Soon far fewer men died. With a lamp and a chart, one woman saved拯救 countless lives, and showed the world that good numbers can change everything.
参 考 译 文
一百五十多年前，一位勇敢的英国女子——弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔，成为了一名护士。战争期间，她被派往一所挤满伤兵的医院。每到夜里，她提着一盏小小的油灯，在病床之间巡视，人们便称她为「提灯女神」。她很快发现了一件奇怪的事：死于疾病的士兵，远比死于伤口的更多。当时医院肮脏不堪，水质糟糕、空气污浊。南丁格尔坚信干净的病房能让士兵活下来，可几乎没人肯听。于是她做了一件新鲜事：她统计了每一例死亡，画出一张色彩分明的图表。图表让真相一目了然——大多数人死于疾病，而非战伤。这幅图帮她证明了自己的观点，官员们终于动手清洁了医院。很快，死去的人就少多了。凭着一盏灯和一张图，这位女子挽救了无数生命，也向世界证明：好的数据，能改变一切。
