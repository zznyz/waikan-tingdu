Built Out of a Promise
0:000:50
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Beside a wide river in northern北部的 India stands a building made of white marble. It was not built for a king to live in. It was built because of a promise承诺. When the emperor皇帝 lost his wife, he decided决定 to give her the most beautiful resting place the world had ever seen. Work began in 1632 and took more than twenty years. Twenty thousand workers and a thousand elephants大象 carried stone from far away. The builders set tiny极小的 pieces of coloured stone into the marble to make flowers that never die. The whole building is a perfect square, so it looks exactly the same from every side. Its colour changes with the light: pink at dawn黎明, white at noon, golden under the moon. Today about seven million visitors参观者 come every year to look at what love and sadness built together, and most of them go quiet without knowing why.
参 考 译 文
在印度北部一条宽阔的河边，立着一座用白色大理石造的建筑。它不是给国王住的，它是因为一句承诺才被造出来的。当那位皇帝失去妻子时，他决定为她建一座世上最美的安息之所。工程 1632 年动工，修了二十多年。两万名工人和一千头大象从很远的地方把石头运来。工匠把一片片细小的彩色石头嵌进大理石里，拼成永远不会枯萎的花。整座建筑是一个完美的正方形，所以从任何一面看过去都一模一样。它的颜色随着光线变化：黎明时是粉的，正午是白的，月光下是金的。今天每年约有七百万游客来看这座由爱与悲伤共同造起来的建筑，而他们大多会不由自主地安静下来。
