The Fleet That Sailed West

Nearly ninety years before Columbus crossed the Atlantic Ocean, a Chinese sailor水手 named Zheng He set out to the west with the largest fleet舰队 the world had ever seen. His first voyage航行 in 1405 carried more than two hundred ships and nearly thirty thousand men. The biggest of those ships were several几个的 times longer than the little boats that later sailed out of Europe. Between 1405 and 1433 he made seven voyages. He reached Southeast Asia, India, the Middle East and the east coast of Africa, and he brought home silk, spices香料, medicine and once a tall animal with a very long neck, which surprised the whole capital首都. He did not go to take land. He went to trade贸易, to carry gifts and to show how far Chinese ships could travel. Then the voyages stopped and the great ships were left to rot, but the sea routes路线 he opened stayed in use.

在哥伦布横渡大西洋的将近九十年前，一位名叫郑和的中国航海家，带着当时世界上最大的船队向西出发。1405 年的第一次航行，带了两百多艘船、近三万人。其中最大的船，比后来从欧洲驶出的那些小船长好几倍。从 1405 到 1433 年，他一共出海七次。他到过东南亚、印度、中东和非洲东海岸，带回丝绸、香料、药材，还有一次带回一只脖子极长的高大动物，让整个京城都吃了一惊。他不是去占地的。他是去做贸易、去送礼、去让人看看中国的船能开多远。后来航行停了，那些大船被丢在那里烂掉，但他开出来的那些航线，一直有人在走。
