The Word in the Headline That Nobody Read
0:001:25
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
On a December morning in 2022, a laboratory in California announced宣布 that it had got more energy out of a fusion reaction than it had put in. The headlines said breakeven at last. The experiment was real. One hundred and ninety-two lasers were fired at a fuel capsule the size of a peppercorn, and the fuel inside it burned. About two megajoules of laser light went in; about three came out. For the first time a reaction of this kind had produced产生 more than it took to start it. Now read that sentence again and notice the word in. It means the light that reached the capsule. It does not mean the electricity that made the light. The lasers are not efficient高效的, and the building around them is not free. Counted from the power supply, the shot consumed消耗 something like a hundred times what it returned. Nothing was gained获得; something was proved. That distinction is the whole story. Every claim of net energy carries a boundary that is not always stated, and the answer changes depending on where the line is drawn. What remains剩下 is not small. A power station would have to repeat重复 that shot about ten times a second, not once a day; the lasers would have to become far more efficient; the fuel would have to be made as fast as it is burned; and the walls would have to survive承受住 all of it. None of that makes the morning less real. It only makes the headline shorter than the truth.
参 考 译 文
2022 年 12 月的一个早晨，加利福尼亚的一间实验室宣布：他们从一次聚变反应里取出的能量，比投进去的还多。新闻标题说，收支平衡终于实现了。 实验是真的。一百九十二束激光被打在一个胡椒粒大小的燃料靶丸上，里面的燃料烧了起来。大约两兆焦的激光进去，大约三兆焦出来。这是此类反应第一次放出的能量超过了启动它所需要的能量。 现在请把那句话再读一遍，注意「投进去」这三个字。 它指的是打到靶丸上的光，不是发出这束光所用的电。激光并不高效，围着它的那栋楼也不是白来的。从电源那头算起，这一发消耗掉的，大约是它放出的一百倍。什么也没赚到；但有些东西被证明了。 这个区别，就是这件事的全部。任何关于「净得能量」的说法，都自带一条并不总被说出口的边界；线画在哪里，答案就跟着变。 剩下的问题并不小。一座电站得把这样一发大约每秒重复十次，而不是一天一次；激光得变得高效得多；燃料得以被烧掉的速度被造出来；而器壁还得扛得住这一切。 这些都不会让那个早晨变得不真实。它们只是说明：那条标题，比事实要短。
