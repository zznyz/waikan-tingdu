The Great Butterfly Journey
0:000:50
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Every autumn, millions of orange butterflies leave Canada and begin a long journey旅程 south. These small insects昆虫 fly more than four thousand kilometres to reach one small forest森林 in Mexico. Nobody teaches them the way. Scientists believe they read the sun and the earth itself to find the right direction方向. The strangest part comes next. No single butterfly finishes the whole trip. The ones that fly north again are their children's children, so each new generation一代（人） must find a place it has never seen. Today the butterflies are in danger危险, because people cut down trees and the wild flowers they feed on are disappearing. Scientists and farmers are working hard to protect保护 them, and children in many countries now plant those flowers in their own gardens. Every spring, people still discover发现 the first orange wings coming back.
参 考 译 文
每年秋天，数以百万计的橙色蝴蝶离开加拿大，开始一段向南的漫长旅程。这些小小的昆虫要飞越四千多公里，才能抵达墨西哥的一小片森林。没有谁教过它们路该怎么走。科学家相信，它们靠太阳和大地本身来辨认正确的方向。最奇特的还在后头：没有哪一只蝴蝶能飞完全程。再往北飞回来的，已经是它们孩子的孩子——每一代新生的蝴蝶，都要去寻找一个自己从未见过的地方。今天，这些蝴蝶正处于危险之中：人们砍伐树木，它们赖以为生的野花也在消失。科学家和农民正在努力保护它们，许多国家的孩子如今也在自家花园里种下那些野花。每年春天，人们依然会发现第一批橙色的翅膀，飞了回来。
