In 1960, a young British woman named Jane Goodall travelled to a forest in Africa. She wanted to study wild chimpanzees, our closest animal relatives亲属. Most scientists watched animals from far away, but Jane chose to sit quietly among the trees for hours, day after day. She was patient有耐心的, and slowly the shy害羞的 chimpanzees began to trust信任 her. Instead of giving them numbers, she gave each one a name. One morning, Jane saw something amazing. A chimpanzee picked a thin stick, removed its leaves, and used it to pull tasty insects from a hole. Until then, people believed that only humans could make tools工具. Her careful observation观察 changed how we understand animals. Today, Jane travels the world, teaching young people to protect保护 nature. Her story shows that patience and curiosity can lead to great discoveries发现.

1960 年，一位名叫珍·古道尔的英国年轻女子来到非洲的一片森林。她想研究野生黑猩猩——我们最近的动物亲属。大多数科学家从很远的地方观察动物，珍却选择安静地坐在树丛之间，一坐就是几个小时，日复一日。她很有耐心，害羞的黑猩猩渐渐开始信任她。她没有给它们编号，而是给每一只都起了名字。一天清晨，珍看到了惊人的一幕：一只黑猩猩摘下一根细树枝，捋去叶子，用它从洞里钓出美味的昆虫。在那之前，人们一直相信只有人类才会制造工具。她细致的观察，改变了我们理解动物的方式。今天，珍走遍世界，教年轻人保护自然。她的故事告诉我们：耐心与好奇，能带来伟大的发现。
