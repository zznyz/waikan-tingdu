Using Mosquitoes to Fight Disease
0:001:03
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Dengue is a dangerous disease that mosquitoes spread（使）传播 to millions of people every year. Now scientists have a surprising plan to fight it: release释放 even more mosquitoes! At first, this sounds crazy. But these special mosquitoes carry Wolbachia, a harmless无害的 bacteria细菌 that lives inside many insects. It does not hurt the mosquito at all. Here is the clever part. When a mosquito carries Wolbachia, the dengue virus病毒 can no longer grow well inside it. Scientists breed繁育 these mosquitoes and set them free in wild野生的 neighbourhoods, where they mix with local ones and pass Wolbachia to their young. Slowly, the whole mosquito population becomes safe. Does it really work? In a careful trial in Indonesia, dengue cases fell by about 77%. The evidence证据 is strong. A scary-sounding idea can still be a good one. So when you meet a new technology, do not just fear it — look at the proof, and think for yourself.
