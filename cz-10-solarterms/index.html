The 24 Solar Terms: China's Calendar in the Sky
0:000:59
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Long ago, Chinese farmers looked up at the sky to plan their year. They watched how the sun moved and how the seasons季节 changed. From this, they made the 24 solar terms, an old calendar日历 written in the sky. Each term marks a small change in weather天气 or nature. Together, the terms divide one whole year into 24 equal parts. For thousands of years, they told farmers when to plant their seeds and when to bring in the harvest收获. Every term has its own lovely name. Start of Spring" tells people that warm days are near. Grain Rain" brings soft rain to help young crops农作物 grow. Others speak of insects昆虫 waking, snow falling, or the longest day of the year. Even today, many Chinese people still follow the solar terms in daily life. They shape what people eat and how they farm. In 2016, the 24 solar terms joined the UNESCO list of world heritage遗产, a treasure财富 shared by all.
参 考 译 文
很久以前，中国的农民抬头望向天空，来安排一年的农事。他们观察太阳怎样移动，四季怎样更替。由此，他们创造了二十四节气——一部写在天空中的古老历法。每一个节气，都标记着天气或自然中一处细微的变化。合在一起，这些节气把一整年平均分成二十四份。几千年来，它们告诉农民何时播种、何时收获。每个节气都有自己动听的名字。"立春"告诉人们，温暖的日子近了；"谷雨"带来绵绵细雨，滋润幼小的庄稼生长。还有的节气讲的是昆虫苏醒、大雪纷飞，或是一年中白昼最长的一天。即使在今天，许多中国人在日常生活里依然遵循着节气，它影响着人们吃什么、怎样耕作。2016年，二十四节气入选联合国教科文组织世界非物质文化遗产，成为全人类共享的珍宝。
