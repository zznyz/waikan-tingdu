对话 1

A That's right. I take the children to school.
B Do you get up early in the morning?
A Oh, yes. I have to get up at 6 o'clock.
B And when does school begin?
A It begins at 8 o'clock. We always arrive fifteen minutes earlier.

B Yes. I have a stomachache. It started this morning.
A Did you eat anything bad?
B Maybe. I had some cold food yesterday evening.
A You should go to see a doctor.
B You're right. I'll go after class.

B. At 9 a.m. on Saturday. 周六上午 9 点

对话 1

B Wow! When shall we start?
A I’ll pick you up at about 8:00 tomorrow morning, OK?

A That’s OK. See you then.

第 05 期长对话 · 3 题
🎧 听力音频 先听音频做题，再对答案 · 反复磨耳
1. What are the speakers mainly talking about? 两位说话人主要在谈论什么？
A. How to boil water. 怎样烧水
B. Different kinds of tea. 茶的不同种类
C. How to make tea. 怎样泡茶
2. Who will John go to the village with? John 打算和谁一起去乡下？
A. His friends. 他的朋友
B. His family. 他的家人
C. His teachers. 他的老师
3. What was the biggest challenge for Mr.Smith? 对 Smith 先生来说，最大的挑战是什么？
A. Cold weather. 寒冷的天气
B. Heavy snow. 大雪
C. Thin air. 稀薄的空气
📝 查看答案
1. C   2. B   3. C
📖 查看原文（听力材料）
对话 1
B There are many different kinds of tea in China, such as Longjing and Tieguanyin.
A Yes. They are very delicious. Could you tell me how to make tea?
B Let me show you. First, boil some water.
A Here is water boiled just now.
B OK. Then warm the teapot by pouring in a little boiled water, and then pour it out.
A Shall I put in the tea now? I only have this. It’s from a Chinese friend.
B Yes, it’s green tea. And then pour in the boiled water and wait for two or three minutes. Then you can drink the delicious tea.
A Wow… It smells good.
对话 2
B Qingming Festival is coming soon. Do you have any plans for it, John?
A Yes. I want to go to a quiet and beautiful village with my parents and my sister.
B Why do you choose a village?
A Because the life there is very slow and comfortable.
B What do you like most about the village?
A The fresh air there. What about you, Betty?
B I plan to learn paper cutting this holiday.
A Is it difficult to learn paper cutting?
B It’s a little difficult but very interesting. It is a wonderful traditional Chinese skill.
对话 3
B Welcome to our show, Mr. Smith! Congratulations on climbing Mount Qomolangma last month. What was the climb like?
A It was very hard. The biggest challenge was the thin air. It was difficult to breathe.
B Did the cold weather and heavy snow make it even harder?
A Yes, but with good preparation, we got over the difficulties.
B Glad to hear that! So, what do you think is the most important quality for a climber?
A A strong mind. You must never give up, even when it is really difficult.
B Thank you for sharing your experience with us.
A You’re welcome.

C. It is still a poor place. 那里依然很贫困

📖 查看原文（听力材料）
对话 1
B Oh, there is a jacket on the ground. Whose is this, Mike?
A It must belong to John. He played basketball here just now.
