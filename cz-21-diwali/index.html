Diwali: India's Festival of Lights
0:000:55

英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Every autumn, millions of families in India celebrate庆祝 Diwali, the Festival of Lights. Before the big days, people clean their houses and decorate装饰 the floors with rangoli — bright patterns made of coloured powder. When night falls, they light rows of small oil lamps灯 called diyas and place them by doors and windows. The warm golden light carries one simple idea: light wins over darkness黑暗, and good wins over evil. The festival also tells an old story. Long ago, Prince Rama came home after fourteen hard years away, and people lit thousands of little flames to welcome欢迎 him back. Today, Diwali is still a time for coming home. Families share sweets甜食, wear new clothes and watch fireworks烟花 in the night sky. Friends visit each other with gifts and warm wishes. Wherever the tiny flames are shining, the message is the same: hope希望, family and a bright new start.
参 考 译 文
每年秋天，印度千百万个家庭都会庆祝排灯节——「灯的节日」。节日来临前，人们把屋子打扫干净，在地上用彩色粉末画出明艳的图案，叫作「蓝果丽」。夜幕降临时，他们点亮一排排叫作「迪亚」的小油灯，摆放在门边和窗前。温暖的金色灯光传递着一个朴素的道理：光明战胜黑暗，善良战胜邪恶。这个节日还讲述着一个古老的故事。很久以前，罗摩王子在外漂泊了十四个艰难的年头后回到家乡，人们点起成千上万簇小小的灯火来迎接他归来。今天，排灯节依然是回家团圆的时节。一家人分享甜食、穿上新衣，仰望夜空中的烟花；朋友们带着礼物和温暖的祝福互相走动。无论哪里有小小的灯火在闪亮，传递的讯息都一样：希望、家人，和一个崭新明亮的开始。
