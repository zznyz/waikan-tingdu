The Ballad of Mulan
0:000:42
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Long ago in China, an old soldier received a letter: he must join加入 the army军队 again. His daughter Mulan knew he was too weak虚弱的 to fight, and her little brother was only a child. So she made a brave choice选择. She dressed as a man, bought a horse at the market, and rode away to fight in her father's place. For twelve years Mulan lived and fought as a soldier, and no one discovered her secret秘密. When the war ended, the emperor皇帝 offered her gold and a high position职位, but she asked only for a fast horse to carry her home. Back in her village村庄, she put on her old dress again. When her old friends came to visit, they could hardly believe their eyes: the brave soldier they had followed for years was a woman.
参 考 译 文
很久以前，在中国，一位年迈的老兵收到一纸军书：他必须再次从军。女儿木兰知道，父亲已年老体弱、上不了战场，而弟弟还只是个孩子。于是她做了一个勇敢的选择：女扮男装，去集市买来骏马，替父出征。整整十二年，木兰同将士们一起生活、一起征战，没有人发现她的秘密。战事结束，皇帝要赏她黄金和高官厚禄，她却只求一匹快马送她回乡。回到村庄，她重新穿上旧时的衣裳。昔日的战友前来探望，简直不敢相信自己的眼睛：他们追随多年的勇敢战士，竟是一位女子。
