Dragon Boat Festival
0:000:45
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Every year, on the fifth day of the fifth lunar农历的 month, Chinese people celebrate庆祝 the Dragon Boat Festival. It is over two thousand years old. The story is about Qu Yuan, a famous poet诗人 who loved his country deeply. Long ago, enemies敌人 took over his land. Qu Yuan was so sad that he jumped into a river and drowned溺水. The local people admired敬仰 him. They quickly rowed划(船) their boats out to look for him. They also threw rice into the water, hoping the fish would eat the rice instead of hurting him. This was the beginning of zongzi, the sticky rice dumplings粽子. Today, people still race dragon boats and eat zongzi. In this way, they remember Qu Yuan and his love for his country.
参 考 译 文
每年农历五月初五，中国人都会庆祝端午节——一个有着两千多年历史的节日。这个故事讲的是屈原，一位深爱祖国的著名诗人。很久以前，敌人占领了他的国家；屈原悲伤至极，投江自尽。当地百姓十分敬仰他，急忙划船出去寻找他，还把米饭投入江中，希望鱼儿去吃米饭、而不去伤害他——这便是粽子（糯米团子）的由来。今天，人们依然赛龙舟、吃粽子，用这样的方式纪念屈原，纪念他对祖国的爱。
