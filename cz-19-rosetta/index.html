The Rosetta Stone: Reading a Lost Language
0:000:51

英 文 原 文 · 听 读
More than 200 years ago, soldiers in Egypt dug up a large black stone石头. On it were three kinds of writing. Two were old Egyptian scripts, and the third was ancient古代的 Greek. For over a thousand years, no one in the world could read the beautiful Egyptian pictures, called hieroglyphs. The meaning of this old language语言 had become a complete secret秘密. Then scholars学者 had an idea. The Greek part could still be read, so it might unlock开启 the others. They guessed that the same message was written three times. Slowly, they used the Greek words to match使相配 the Egyptian signs, sound by sound. In 1822, a Frenchman named Champollion finally cracked the code. Suddenly, temples, tombs and letters from long ago could speak again. That one discovery had opened a door to thousands of years of history历史.
参 考 译 文
两百多年前，一些士兵在埃及挖出了一块巨大的黑色石碑。石碑上刻着三种文字：两种是古老的埃及文字，第三种是古希腊文。一千多年来，世界上没有人能读懂那些美丽的埃及图画——它们被称作象形文字。这门古老语言的含义，早已成了一个彻底的秘密。后来，学者们想到了一个办法：希腊文那部分仍然能读，也许能借它解开其余的文字。他们猜想，同一段话被刻了三遍。就这样，他们用希腊词，一个音一个音地去对应埃及符号。1822 年，一位名叫商博良的法国人终于破译了这套密码。刹那间，古老的神庙、陵墓和书信仿佛重新开口说话。那一个发现，为人类打开了一扇通往数千年历史的大门。
