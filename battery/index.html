The Cheaper Battery
0:001:23
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Every time we charge a phone or drive an electric car, we depend on a battery. Most of these batteries are made with lithium, a light metal that can store储存 large amounts of energy in a small space. Yet lithium is not evenly spread across the earth; it is mined in only a few regions, which keeps its price high. As the world's demand需求 for clean energy grows, scientists have begun to ask whether some cheaper material could replace取代 it. One promising alternative替代选择 is sodium, an element found in ordinary salt and in the sea. Because sodium is far more abundant丰富的 than lithium, it is easier and cheaper to obtain almost anywhere. A sodium battery works on much the same principle: tiny particles move back and forth to release and hold energy. In theory, such batteries could ease our reliance on rare resources资源 and make green technology available to more people. However, sodium is not a perfect answer. Sodium batteries tend to be heavier and store less energy for their size, so they are not yet as efficient高效的 as lithium ones. For now, they may suit large fixed systems rather than small devices. Even so, many researchers believe that using such common materials is a more sustainable可持续的 path — one that balances cost, resources and the health of the planet. The search for a cheaper battery, in the end, is also a search for a fairer and cleaner future.
参 考 译 文
每一次给手机充电、开动电动车，我们都在依赖电池。如今大多数电池以锂制成——这种轻金属能把大量能量储存在很小的空间里。然而锂在地球上分布不均，只在少数地区开采，价格居高不下。随着全世界对清洁能源的需求增长，科学家开始追问：有没有更便宜的材料能取代它？钠就是一个颇具前景的替代选择——它存在于普通食盐和海水中。由于钠比锂丰富得多，几乎处处都更廉价易得。钠电池原理相同：微小粒子来回移动，释放并储存能量，减轻我们对稀有资源的依赖。不过钠并不完美：它更重，也不如锂电池高效，因此更适合大型固定式储能，而非小巧设备。即便如此，使用这类常见材料是一条更可持续的道路，通向更公平、更清洁的未来。
