The Day a Word Made Sense
0:000:47
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
Helen Keller was not yet two years old when a serious illness疾病 took away her sight视力 and her hearing听力. She grew up in a dark and silent寂静的 world, unable to tell anyone what she wanted. When she was six, a young teacher named Anne came to her home. At first the child fought against her, but the teacher did not give up. One day, beside the well, Anne let cool water run over Helen's hand and spelled拼写 the word “water” into her open hand again and again. Suddenly突然 the child understood: everything in the world had a name. Before night fell she had learned thirty words. Helen went on to finish college大学, write books and travel around the world, speaking for people who could not see or hear. She once said that although尽管 the world is full of trouble, it is also full of people who beat it.

海伦·凯勒还不满两岁，一场重病就夺走了她的视力和听力。她在一个又黑又静的世界里长大，想要什么也没法告诉任何人。六岁那年，一位名叫安妮的年轻老师来到她家。起初这孩子拼命反抗，但老师没有放弃。有一天，在井边，安妮让清凉的水流过海伦的手，一遍又一遍地把 water 这个词拼写在她摊开的手心里。忽然之间，孩子明白了：世上的每样东西都有一个名字。天黑之前，她已经学会了三十个词。后来海伦读完了大学，写书，走遍世界，为那些看不见、听不到的人发声。她说过，这个世界固然充满苦难，但也同样充满战胜苦难的人。
