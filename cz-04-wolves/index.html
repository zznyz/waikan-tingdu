How Wolves Changed a River
0:000:58
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
For seventy years, no wolves狼（复数 wolves） lived in Yellowstone Park. Hunters猎人 had killed the last ones long ago. Without wolves, the deer had nothing to fear害怕. They grew in number and ate the young trees by the rivers all day. Soon the green banks turned bare光秃的, the soil土壤 fell into the water, and many streams became wide, shallow浅的 and empty of life. Then, in 1995, scientists brought a few grey wolves back from Canada and set them free. The wolves hunted the deer, and the deer grew careful小心的. They no longer ate by the open rivers all day. Free from the hungry deer, the young trees grew tall again. Beavers came back and built dams, making pools where fish and birds could live. Roots held the soil, the banks grew strong, and the rivers came back to life. One change at the top had changed a whole living world below. Yellowstone shows how one animal can shape塑造 a river.
参 考 译 文
整整七十年，黄石公园里再没有狼。很久以前，猎人就把最后几只狼杀光了。没有了狼，鹿群便无所畏惧。它们越来越多，整天啃食河边的小树。很快，绿色的河岸变得光秃秃，泥土滑落进水里，许多溪流变得又宽又浅，失去了生机。后来，1995年，科学家从加拿大带回几只灰狼，把它们放归了公园。狼开始捕猎鹿群，鹿也变得警觉起来，不再整天待在开阔的河边吃草。少了饥饿鹿群的啃食，水边的小树重新长高了。河狸也回来了，它们筑起水坝，形成一个个水塘，鱼和鸟都能在这里栖息。树根抓住泥土，河岸变得结实，河流慢慢恢复了生机。原来，食物链顶端的一个变化，竟能重塑下面整个生命世界。黄石公园让我们看到：一种动物，也能塑造一条河流。
