英 文 原 文 · 听 读
For twenty-seven years Nelson Mandela was a prisoner of the South African state. He had been jailed for his part in the struggle斗争 against apartheid, a system of laws that divided people by race and denied the black majority almost every equal平等的 right. When he walked free in February 1990, he was seventy-one, and much of the country expected what usually follows such a history: revenge报复. It did not come. Mandela argued that a nation could not be built on punishment. Four years later he was elected president in the country's first election open to voters of all races. Rather than put the old rulers on trial, his government created the Truth and Reconciliation Commission, which offered amnesty to those who confessed to politically motivated crimes — but only if they told the whole truth in public. Victims witnessed见证 the men who had hunted their families describe, calmly, what they had done. Many South Africans felt that justice正义 had been traded away. Mandela's reply was that only a shared record could unite团结 people who had been taught to fear one another. His forgiveness宽恕 was never softness: what changed after his release释放 was not his belief in the cause, but his judgement about what would hold a country together.

有二十七年，纳尔逊·曼德拉是南非政府的囚徒。他入狱，是因为参与了反对种族隔离制度的斗争——那是一套按种族把人分开的法律，几乎剥夺了占人口多数的黑人的一切平等的权利。1990 年 2 月他走出监狱时已经七十一岁，而这个国家的许多人都在等待这种历史通常带来的结果：报复。报复没有来。曼德拉坚持：一个国家不可能建立在惩罚之上。四年后，他在南非第一次向所有种族的选民开放的大选中当选总统。他的政府没有把旧统治者送上审判席，而是成立了“真相与和解委员会”——向交代出于政治动机之罪行的人提供特赦，但前提是必须当众把全部真相说出来。受害者见证那些追捕过他们家人的人平静地讲述自己做过什么。许多南非人觉得，正义被拿去做了交易。曼德拉的回应是：只有一份共同的记录，才能把那些被教着彼此恐惧的人团结起来。他的宽恕从来不是软弱：释放之后改变的，不是他对那项事业的信念，而是他对“究竟什么才真的能让一个国家不散架”的判断。
