The Green Sea Turtle Comes Back
0:001:01
▶ 念到哪句，哪句高亮
英 文 原 文 · 听 读
The green sea turtle is one of the largest turtles in the ocean海洋. For millions of years, these gentle温和的 animals have swum through warm seas and laid their eggs on quiet beaches. But not long ago, they were in great danger. People hunted them for their meat and shells壳. Bright lights and busy hotels took over the beaches where they nested. Many turtles also ate floating plastic塑料 bags, thinking they were food. Their numbers grew smaller and smaller. Then people decided to help. Many countries made new laws法律 to stop the hunting捕猎. Quiet beaches were kept safe, so mother turtles could lay their eggs in peace. Volunteers志愿者 guarded the nests and led baby turtles safely to the sea. People also worked hard to keep the ocean clean. Slowly, the plan began to work. In many places, the number of green sea turtles is rising again. Their return shows a happy truth: when we protect保护 nature, nature can come back.
参 考 译 文
绿海龟是海洋里最大的海龟之一。数百万年来，这些温和的动物在温暖的海水中游弋，把卵产在安静的沙滩上。可就在不久前，它们陷入了巨大的危险。人们为了龟肉和龟壳猎捕它们；明亮的灯光和喧闹的酒店，占据了它们筑巢的沙滩；许多海龟还把漂浮的塑料袋当成食物吞下。它们的数量一年比一年少。后来，人们决定伸出援手。许多国家制定了新的法律，禁止猎捕。安静的沙滩被保护起来，好让龟妈妈安心产卵。志愿者守护着龟巢，把小海龟安全地送入大海。人们也努力保持海洋洁净。慢慢地，办法开始奏效。在许多地方，绿海龟的数量又重新回升。它们的归来告诉我们一个美好的道理：当我们保护自然，自然也会回来。
